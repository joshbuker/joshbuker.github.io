# Progress Web Applications (PWAs) are a Dead Technology

And google killed them.

https://github.com/beverloo/notification-triggers/issues/7
[PWA Notes](obsidian://open?vault=notes&file=Evergreen%20Notes%2FProgressive%20Web%20Applications)

Ongoing legal battles with play store, Google and Apple are trying to keep their revenue from app store integration. See Epic Games.

A less known aspect - PWAs, the technology to use web as a direct replacement for native applications, are intentionally being stunted such that app developers cannot use them to compete, drawing users away from the walled garden and juicy 30% cut of every sale.
