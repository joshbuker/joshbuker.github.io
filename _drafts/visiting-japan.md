Recently, I went to Japan with my partner, Rebecca.

## Tokyo (Arrival)

![Vending Machine](/assets/images/posts/japan/First%20Vending%20Machine.jpg){: .w-25.right.rounded-10 }

We started off our trip by flying into Tokyo, and to try and spare ourselves trying to figure out how to get to Fukuoka immediately after our 10ish hour flight, we decided to spend a day in Tokyo recovering before taking a domestic flight down.

We encountered our first world famous Japanese vending machine (ignoring the many we probably passed in the airport, anyway) at our hotel.

Making a plan to circle back to the vending machine once we were done, we set out for a short walk around the area to see what it was like.

![Country Tiles](/assets/images/posts/japan/tiles.jpg){: .rounded-10.left.w-25 }

The area around our hotel appeared to be mostly industrial, but there were some interesting art tiles on the pathway, representing many countries around the world with animals famous to their respective regions. We had fun practicing our Katakana, reading which country each animal represented.

Our first day in Tokyo wasn't the most eventful, but the staff at the hotel were kind enough to help us plan a route to Haneda airport, and we made our way to our next stop, Fukuoka, without incident.
